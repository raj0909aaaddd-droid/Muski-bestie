✦   the letter I never knew how to begin

Dear Muski, 🌙

I've started this a hundred times. Each time, the words felt too small — too ordinary for something that lives in me like light lives in old glass. ✨ So let me not try to be perfect. Let me just be honest.

You are one of the few people in this life who made me feel understood without explanation. Not because you always knew what to say — but because you stayed. Even in the silences. Especially in the silences. 🤍

There are places inside a person that cannot be reached through ordinary conversation — through small talk and surface smiles and all the polite distance we keep between ourselves and the world. You somehow always found those places. Not by force. But by simply being soft enough that the walls forgot why they were built. 🌿

I think about the kind of warmth you carry. 🕯️ The kind that doesn't announce itself. The kind that you only notice after you've left — when the room suddenly feels colder, when the air feels thinner, and you realize something essential had been there all along and now it isn't. That's what your presence is. Essential.

There are memories I hold that don't belong to any specific hour or place. They're just feelings — the colour of a certain afternoon, the weight of a conversation that changed something quiet in me, the way certain moments stretched themselves wide enough to hold everything I hadn't been able to carry alone. You were in all of those. 🌅

I want you to know that you have made this existence softer. 🌸 Not easier — life is what it is. But softer. The way snow makes the world quieter without changing anything real about it. You are that. The softness that makes the sharp things bearable.

And I know that most of this I've never said out loud. ✦ That's partly why this exists — because there are things that deserve to exist outside of my chest, even if they're only ever read once, even if this whole page disappears and only the feeling remains. The feeling is the real thing anyway.

I am grateful for you. 💛 Not in a transactional way — not because of what you did or gave or offered — but for the simple, extraordinary fact that you exist. That somewhere in this enormous and often indifferent world, you are here. Breathing. Feeling things deeply. Being you. That alone is something I find inexplicably beautiful.

If I could give you one thing, it would be this: the knowledge — felt in the bones, not just thought in the mind — that you are deeply, completely, genuinely seen. 🌙 Not the version of you that shows up when you're trying. The whole version. All the parts. Even the ones you keep behind your eyes when you're sitting in a room full of people and still feel a little alone. I see that part too. And I hold it gently. Always. 🤍

Thank you for existing in my life. ✨ That sentence sounds small. It isn't. It contains multitudes.

— with everything quiet and full, always 🕊️

01 🕯️

The Warmth That Stayed

There are conversations that end but never really leave. They settle somewhere under the ribs, warm like something lit from the inside. That's what talking to you felt like — always.

02 🌙

The Comfortable Quiet

Most people fill silence with noise. You let it breathe. There was something rare about being near you and not needing to perform — just existing without explanation, without apology.

03 🌅

The In-Between Hours

Not the big moments. The small ones. The unremarkable hours that turned out to be the ones I held onto most. The way light fell a certain way. The feeling of time slowing down, not because anything special happened — but because you were in it, and everything around you felt worth noticing.

04 🌿

The Safety of You

Some people make you feel like you have to be careful. With you, I felt the opposite — like I could be unfinished. Like being incomplete was allowed. That kind of safety is rare. It's a gift people don't know they give.

05 ✨

How You Listened

Not waiting for your turn to speak. Actually listening. Like what I said mattered before it was finished — like I mattered before I'd proven anything. I don't think you know how uncommon that is.

06 💫

The Texture of Your Presence

It's difficult to explain the texture of someone's presence — the specific atmosphere they bring into a space. Yours was warm, unhurried, lived-in. Like a room that has known laughter for years. Like something that held its shape even when everything else shifted around it.

07 🤍

Seen Without Saying

There were times you understood something about me that I hadn't yet found words for. That kind of being seen — without having to perform your own feelings — settles into you. It stays long after the moment passes.

08 🌸

Your Softness as Strength

The world mistakes softness for weakness. But you were proof it isn't. To choose warmth when hardness would be easier — that takes something. That takes a kind of quiet courage I have always admired in you.
